Coaching

Improving, step by step

My goal: to help players better understand their game, identify their strengths and their areas for improvement. Scroll to discover the journey.

The method

Three principles, one single goal: your progress

Consistency

One-off video feedback is not enough. It is follow-up over time that makes it possible to observe progress and adjust the work.

Quality

Feedback must be precise, understandable and adapted to the player. Too much information, too long or too general, can make the message difficult to use.

Understanding

The goal is not simply to show players what they did. It is to help them understand why they did it and how they can develop their game.

The journey

The follow-up offered

Discussion

A discussion to understand your context and your goals, in order to personalise the follow-up.

Analysis

Viewing of your matches, data and video editing for a complete analysis.

Presentation

Video call, written report and presentation of the final result, with a discussion to develop your understanding of the game.

Action plan

Concrete actions and objectives to apply in training and in matches to improve.
